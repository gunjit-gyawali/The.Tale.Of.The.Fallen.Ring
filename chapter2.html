Chapter 2 - The Shattered World

A long time ago, the world wasn't whole, but it was in harmony. Six great lands floated above an endless sea, massive islands suspended in the sky like drifting continents. They weren't named by where they sat in the world, but by what they were each one holding a piece of existence itself.

The Land of the Present: vibrant, bustling, the heart of culture, trade, and balance.
The Land of the Past: steeped in tradition, cloaked in ancient history and long forgotten wisdom.
The Land of the Future: a place of progress and invention, where ambition pushed the edge of what was possible.
The Land of Heaven: serene and still, closest to the sky, where spirits whispered and prayers carried weight.
The Land of Hell: wild, untamed, a raw surge of chaos and magic unbound.
The Land of Power: the core of strength, where warriors and mages of legend were born and where greatness was forged.

In this world, magic wasn't rare. It was life. It flowed through the air like wind, shimmered in the water, flickered in fire. Children grew up learning to control it. Adults lived by it. Entire nations thrived on it.

And above them all stood the Archons six rulers, each the embodiment of their land's essence. They were more than leaders. They were guardians. Symbols of peace. Anchors of balance.

For centuries, the lands coexisted. Separate, but connected. Different, but united.

Until the sky bled.

Fifteen years ago, it came without warning. Meteors, massive and burning, tore through the clouds like falling gods. One struck each land.

From their craters crawled things that looked like humans but weren't. They walked upright, spoke words that twisted the air, wore stolen skin like costumes. But their blood was black. Their eyes hollow. Their magic, wrong.

And with each meteor came a commander. A creature of nightmares, leading armies of star-born monsters.

The Archons fought. Of course they did. They stood tall, wielded their ancient power, and tried to protect their people.

But one by one, they fell.

One by one, the lands were overrun.

Until only one remained the Land of Power.

There, the final Archon made his stand. They say he was the strongest of them all. He fought their leader for twenty days and twenty nights, the sky itself screaming under the weight of their clash.

And in the end, he made the ultimate sacrifice.

He poured his life, his magic, his very soul into a seal. A colossal barrier that wrapped around the entire island locking the invaders inside, and buying the rest of the world a sliver of time to survive.

But survival came at a cost.

The lands, once connected, sealed themselves off. Borders closed. Travel forbidden. Communication lost. Trust shattered. Nations turned inward, ruled not by hope, but fear. Monsters stalked the shadows. And the name "Archon"? It became little more than a myth.

The world didn't die. But peace did.

Present Day - Kiyoshi Aetherius

Fifteen years have passed.

The Land of the Present is quiet now but it's not peaceful. Magic still exists, but it's tightly controlled. People don't talk about the meteors. They don't talk about the monsters. They definitely don't talk about the Archons.

They live. They survive. And they try not to remember.

On the edge of a forest road, under a sky heavy with clouds, a boy walks alone.

His name is Kiyoshi Aetherius. Seventeen years old. Eyes like a storm quiet but always thinking. In his hand, he holds something odd: a ring. Worn. Old. Almost ancient.

He found it not long ago, deep in the woods where no one goes. He doesn't know why he picked it up.

He just had to.

He doesn't know why it whispers when the world is still.

He doesn't know why dreams feel heavier now.

But ever since he touched it, something inside him shifted like a door unlocked in the back of his mind.

The wind stills. The sky darkens. And the ring begins to glow.

Kiyoshi doesn't know it yet, but he's just stepped into a war that the world has tried to forget.

He doesn't know it yet, but his blood carries the echo of the Archons.

He doesn't know it yet, but someone from beyond has seen him.

And far, far away, on an island sealed for fifteen years...

The barrier is beginning to crack.
